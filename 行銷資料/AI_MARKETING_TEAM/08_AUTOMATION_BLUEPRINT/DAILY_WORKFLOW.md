# DAILY_WORKFLOW

1. Boss fills daily command.
2. NORA creates tasks.
3. Agents generate drafts into 05_DRAFT_OUTBOX/YYYY-MM-DD/.
4. DR_LAW reviews.
5. NORA writes summary to PENDING_REVIEW.
6. Human approves/rejects/requests rewrite.
7. Approved items can be exported manually. No platform publishing occurs.
