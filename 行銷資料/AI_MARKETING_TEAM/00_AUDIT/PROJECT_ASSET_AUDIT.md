## High-Risk Medical Claims

High-risk filenames/topics observed include `適應症_風溼筋痛`, `止痛止癢`, `感冒頭痛`, `刀傷創傷`, `中暑暈眩`, `肚痛腹脹`, `舟車暈浪`, `蚊蟲叮咬`. Treat them as high-risk in outbound marketing unless owner and legal review confirm label-approved use.

Avoid treatment, cure, root-cause repair, anti-inflammatory cure, cartilage repair, bone spur removal, disc herniation treatment, neuralgia treatment, meridian opening, detox, guaranteed results, instant cure, miracle and special-effect claims.
