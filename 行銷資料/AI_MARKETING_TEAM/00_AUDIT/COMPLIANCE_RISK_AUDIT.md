# COMPLIANCE_RISK_AUDIT

## Default Rule
All external copy must stay inside conservative external-use language: local temporary relief, massage relaxation, daily care, correct use and user experience. No draft may be published directly.

## Status Levels

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | Conservative, no disease/treatment promise, safety boundary present where needed. | Keep in pending human review. |
| NEEDS_REWRITE | Risk implication, vague overclaim, missing warning or aggressive result language. | Rewrite before approval. |
| REJECTED | Disease treatment, cure, guaranteed effect, medical superiority or prohibited condition claim. | Do not use. |

## Mandatory Checks
1. Does the copy claim disease treatment?
2. Does it promise cure, guaranteed outcome or instant effect?
3. Does it imply effects beyond external temporary relief?
4. Does it mention bone spur, arthritis, disc herniation, neuralgia, detox, meridians, cartilage repair or anti-inflammatory cure?
5. Does it omit safety boundaries?

## Approved Safer Claim Zone
局部舒緩、暫時舒緩、按摩放鬆、日常保養、肌肉痠痛、肩頸緊繃、腰背痠、運動後不適、久站後不適、輕微關節不適。

## Publishing Gate
Every generated item must enter `06_REVIEW_QUEUE/PENDING_REVIEW.md`. Only a human reviewer can approve content for manual export.
