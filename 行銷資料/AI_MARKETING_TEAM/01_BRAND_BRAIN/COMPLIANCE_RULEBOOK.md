# COMPLIANCE_RULEBOOK

## Must Prefer
暫時舒緩、局部舒緩、按摩放鬆、日常保養、肌肉痠痛、肩頸緊繃、腰背痠、運動後不適、久站後不適、輕微關節不適。

## Must Not Use
治療、根治、消炎治本、修復軟骨、消除骨刺、治療椎間盤突出、治療神經痛、打通經絡、排毒、改善血液循環到醫療效果、免看醫生、保證有效、一擦就好、神奇、特效。

## Safety Boundaries
- External use only.
- Do not apply to wounds, eyes, mucosa, eczema or sensitive skin.
- Stop use if irritation occurs.
- Children, pregnant users, aspirin/salicylate-sensitive users and chronic disease patients should ask a professional before use.
- Do not combine with heat packs or tight bandaging unless the approved label explicitly allows it.
