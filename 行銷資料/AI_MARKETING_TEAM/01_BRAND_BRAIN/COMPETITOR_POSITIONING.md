# COMPETITOR_POSITIONING

## Differentiation Hypothesis
Do not compete by louder medical claims. Compete by trust: clear ingredients, controllable balm use, practical massage routine, safer wording and local-use scenarios.

## Competitor Buckets
Traditional wide-mouth jars, patches, roll-ons, generic low-price herbal balms and pharmacy-branded external analgesics.

## Safe Angles
Balm versus patch, ingredient sensory experience, daily care after work/sports/driving/temple activity.

## Avoid
Do not claim stronger treatment than competitors, attack competitors, or name competitors in unsupported comparison copy.
