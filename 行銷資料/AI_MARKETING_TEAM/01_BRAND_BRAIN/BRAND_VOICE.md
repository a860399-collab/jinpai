# BRAND_VOICE

## Brand Personality
Professional, Taiwan-local, grounded, clear and practical. The voice can have a little 江湖味, but must never sound vulgar, mystical, miraculous or medically exaggerated.

## Use This Tone
- Professional but not cold.
- Everyday Taiwan situations: service work, driving, labor, sports, temple events and family care.
- Correct external use and safety reminders.
- Local temporary relief, massage relaxation and daily care.
- Trust through clear ingredients and practical usage, not magic.

## Avoid This Tone
神效、保證有效、治百病、老祖宗秘方治好、打通經絡、排毒、活血化瘀治本、治療骨刺、治療退化性關節炎、治療椎間盤突出、治療神經痛。

## Copy Style
Write like a careful local shop owner who respects both the customer and the law: direct, useful and steady.
