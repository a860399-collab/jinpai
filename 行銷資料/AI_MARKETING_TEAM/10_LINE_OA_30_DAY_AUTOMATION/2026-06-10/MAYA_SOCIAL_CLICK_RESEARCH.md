# MAYA_SOCIAL_CLICK_RESEARCH

## Research Scope
Public web search was used to inspect accessible product/category information and current platform constraints. Direct private Facebook/IG/TikTok analytics were not accessible without login, so findings are expressed as safe, testable content patterns rather than unverifiable performance claims.

## Observed Category Context
- Comparable category includes one-strip-root products, topical balms, patches and external relief products.
- Search-visible product/category pages commonly emphasize product form, daily use scenario, ingredient/sensory feel, correct use and trust proof.
- Public social search results are limited; therefore content strategy should be measured through our own LINE OA CTR tests.

## High-Click Pattern Hypotheses To Test
1. Situation-first hook: `坐一整天，肩頸腰背也該下班`
2. Step-based education: `下班後 3 步驟：清潔、取適量、局部按摩`
3. Product-form contrast: `不想貼布悶？油膏可控制用量`
4. Safety trust: `外用保養，這些地方不要擦`
5. Offer-soft CTA: `看今日組合與正確使用方式`
6. Persona-specific hook: `上班族／司機／久站族／廟會出陣後`

## LINE OA Message Structure Recommendation
- Title: 12-18 Chinese characters, situation-first.
- Body: 60-100 Chinese characters, one situation + one use step + one safety line.
- Button: 4-8 characters, action-oriented but not pushy.
- CTA examples: 看使用提醒, 看今日組合, 加入保養清單, 看正確用法.

## What To Avoid
- Miracle language.
- Pain cure promise.
- Disease names.
- Before/after medical proof.
- Overly long LINE push text.
- Discount-only messaging every day.

## Measurement Plan
Track: send date, theme, audience, hook type, button text, image type, open/click count, LINE add/friend count, order notes, compliance status.
