# AMBER_FIRST_7_DAYS_LINE_VISUAL_DIRECTION

Status: PENDING_OWNER_FINAL_APPROVAL
Scope: First 7 days of LINE OA 30-day plan
Goal: Make posts feel like current consumer-friendly content, not traditional hard-selling ads.

## Amber Creative Rule
LINE OA 圖文不要做成「藥膏促銷海報」。要像朋友傳來的實用提醒：一眼看懂情境、一秒知道自己被說中、三秒知道怎麼做。

## Visual System
- Format A: LINE OA rich message / image card, 1040x1040.
- Format B: LINE feed / social square, 1080x1080.
- Format C: Reels/TikTok extension, 1080x1920.
- Typeface direction: clean bold headline + smaller safety line.
- Color: pharmacy white, warm off-white, deep green, restrained gold, black text.
- Image mood: lived-in Taiwan daily life, not hospital, not dramatic pain, not discount-only.
- Product role: visible but secondary; the story leads, product supports.

## Day 1｜2026-06-11｜上班族：肩頸腰背，也該下班了

### Consumer Insight
上班族不想被教育得太正式，他們想看到「這就是我今天」。

### LINE Image Card
- Main headline: 肩頸腰背，也該下班了
- Micro copy: 坐太久，回家先做局部按摩放鬆
- Safety line: 外用，避開傷口、眼口與敏感部位
- Layout: 左 65% 辦公桌/筆電/下班姿態；右下角產品小露出；底部安全提醒。
- Suggested asset: `照片/久坐上班族.jpg` + `照片/產品主圖.jpg`

### Rich Message Button
- Button text: 看使用提醒
- Button style: deep green background, white text, no flashing sale badge.

### 9:16 Extension
0-3s 關筆電：坐一整天了嗎？
3-8s 回家放包：肩頸腰背也該下班
8-15s 取適量：少量開始
15-25s 局部按摩：按摩放鬆
25-30s 安全提醒

### Do Not Use
紅色痛點圈、骨骼圖、醫療診間、治療前後對比。

## Day 7｜2026-06-17｜安全教育：這些地方，外用膏不要擦

### Consumer Insight
安全教育最容易無聊，要做成「保存型 checklist」，不是恐嚇。

### LINE Image Card
- Main headline: 這些地方，外用膏不要擦
- Micro copy: 傷口、眼口、黏膜、敏感部位要避開
- Safety line: 不適持續或加重，請諮詢專業人員
- Layout: 清楚 checklist + 產品小圖 + 安全圖示；白底高可讀性。
- Suggested asset: `照片/產品主圖.jpg` + `照片/使用方式1_取適量塗抹.jpg`

### Rich Message Button
- Button text: 看安全提醒

### 9:16 Extension
0-3s 大字：這些地方不要擦
3-8s 傷口
8-13s 眼口/黏膜
13-18s 敏感部位
18-25s 不適持續請詢問專業人員
25-30s 保存提醒

### Do Not Use
恐嚇口吻、醫療警告圖、紅腫傷口實拍。

## Production Checklist
- [ ] Safety line is visible, not hidden in tiny text.
- [ ] Product does not dominate the whole visual.
- [ ] No disease, cure, guarantee, meridian, detox or inflammation-cure implication.
- [ ] Image source checked against SAFE_IMAGE_REGISTRY.
- [ ] CTA remains low-pressure.
- [ ] Owner final approval before scheduling.


## Locked Safety Line Rule
The daily Safety line is not optional and must not be removed or rewritten by designers. It may be typeset smaller than the headline, but it must remain readable and unobstructed.

## Future Asset Update Rule
If the owner adds new lifestyle photos, update AI_MARKETING_TEAM/01_BRAND_BRAIN/SAFE_IMAGE_REGISTRY.md before using them in LINE OA designs.
