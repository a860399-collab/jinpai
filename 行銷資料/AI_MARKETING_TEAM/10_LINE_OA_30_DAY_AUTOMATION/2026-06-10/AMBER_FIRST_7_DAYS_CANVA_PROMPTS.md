# AMBER_FIRST_7_DAYS_CANVA_PROMPTS

Status: PENDING_OWNER_FINAL_APPROVAL
Purpose: Prompts for Canva/designer/AI image workflow. Do not publish directly.

## Shared Design Prompt
Create a clean Taiwan-local LINE OA image card for an external daily-care balm brand. The design should feel like a useful lifestyle reminder, not a hard-sell medical ad. Use pharmacy white, warm off-white, deep green, restrained gold and black text. Keep the safety reminder readable. Do not show wounds, red pain areas, X-rays, doctors, before/after cure comparison, meridian/detox imagery, or exaggerated medical results.

## Day 1 Prompt
Office worker after closing a laptop, relaxed home/desk setting, product small in lower-right corner, headline: 「肩頸腰背，也該下班了」, subcopy: 「坐太久，回家先做局部按摩放鬆」, safety line: 「外用，避開傷口、眼口與敏感部位」.

## Day 2 Prompt
Service worker after a long standing shift, shoes placed aside, calm seated moment, product on table, headline: 「久站下班，小腿該休息了」, subcopy: 「取適量，局部按摩放鬆」, safety line visible.

## Day 3 Prompt
Driver or salesperson after a long route, warm home arrival or rest-stop mood, product supporting not dominating, headline: 「長途久坐，腰背也要放鬆」, no nerve/disc/pain medical imagery.

## Day 4 Prompt
Post-exercise daily-care routine, towel and sports bag, clean energetic look, headline: 「運動後，別忘了局部放鬆」, checklist style: 休息、伸展、局部按摩.

## Day 5 Prompt
Labor worker after finishing work, respectful and grounded, gloves/tools in corner, product visible, headline: 「搬完重物，先讓身體收工」, avoid injury or suffering imagery.

## Day 6 Prompt
Temple activity after-event care mood, respectful local cultural tone, no deity endorsement, product in personal care corner, headline: 「出陣後，肩頸腰背要照顧」, safety line: 「不誇大、不神化，依標示使用」.

## Day 7 Prompt
Checklist-style safety education card, clean white background, simple icons for wounds/eyes/mucosa/sensitive skin with avoid marks, headline: 「這些地方，外用膏不要擦」, product small, no wound photos.


## Day 7 Execution Note
Use simple consistent icons for wounds, eyes/mouth, mucosa and sensitive skin. Do not use photos of injuries or medical warning imagery. Keep the same warm pharmacy-white/deep-green visual tone as the first six days.
